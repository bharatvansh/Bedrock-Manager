# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about Bedrock Manager.

---

### Is Bedrock Manager free?
Yes, completely free. There is no paywall, subscription, or premium tier. Every feature is available to everyone, forever.

### Is it safe to install?
Bedrock Manager runs entirely on your PC and is built for the community. It only touches the packs and worlds you choose to manage, and never modifies your Minecraft account.

### Which versions of Windows are supported?
Windows 10 and 11, on both x64 and ARM64 hardware. Download the matching installer for your architecture from the [official website](https://bedrockmanager.app).

### Does it work with the Microsoft Store version of Minecraft?
Yes. Bedrock Manager works with Minecraft Bedrock Edition installed from the Microsoft Store on Windows.

### How do I add addons?
Drag and drop your `.mcpack`, `.mcaddon`, or `.mcworld` files straight into the application window. Bedrock Manager sorts them into the right place and lets you toggle them on or off.

### Will I lose my worlds if something goes wrong?
No. You can back up worlds and packs to Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive and restore them anytime, so your builds stay safe across reinstalls and devices.

### Is this an official Minecraft or Mojang product?
No. Bedrock Manager is an independent community tool. It is not approved by or associated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.
